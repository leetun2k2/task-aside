## Verified Runtime Invariants
1. **Single Writer per Canonical Checkout:** Multiple tasks referencing the same repo (including symlinks, relative paths, and case aliases) are strictly serialized with 0ms overlap. Independent repositories progress concurrently.
2. **Verified Stop Before Lock Release:** Lock is never released solely on timer/heartbeat expiration or unverified caller statements. Process tree inspection and explicit stop proof (`process_group_empty`) are mandatory.
3. **Quarantine on Uncertainty:** Adapter crash, missing process stop verification, or communication failure immediately moves the run to `uncertain` and marks the working tree `quarantined`, blocking further claims until administrative supervisor intervention.
4. **Capacity Enforcement:** Maximum 8 active execution slots globally. When all 8 slots are occupied, additional tasks remain in `todo` queue and resume upon slot release.
